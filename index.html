About FUZE

About FUZE.

FUZE is a transformative force with over 20 years of trailblazing excellence, FUZE stands as an integrated industry powerhouse, mastering the art and science of BTL, marketing communications, events & exhibitions, construction, contracting, and interior solutions.

We empower corporate giants and B2C brands alike enabling them to expand, engage, and etch enduring legacies into the fabric of their industries.

Founded in Cairo, 2004.

Founded in the heart of Cairo, Egypt in 2004 by the visionary Ahmed Mahran, Founder & CEO, FUZE was born with a fierce determination to reshape the industry landscape. What began as a bold ambition quickly evolved into a benchmark of excellence a movement that continues to redefine boundaries across Egypt and beyond.

The journey accelerated when Wael El Shafie joined forces as Managing Partner, amplifying FUZE’s momentum. This partnership became a powerful catalyst driving innovation, unlocking new growth dimensions, and fortifying our path to enduring global impact.

Rooted in purpose and ignited by passion, FUZE is committed to transforming the future of corporate brands and B2C clients across all sectors not only in Egypt, but across the region and far beyond its borders. We don’t follow trends. We engineer revolutions.

Step into the world of FUZE.

Rooted in bold innovation and inspired by the timeless harmony of Asian culture, our journey is one of flow, form, and fierce determination. Where bold meets balanced, and business becomes art.
